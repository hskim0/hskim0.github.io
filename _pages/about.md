Welcome! I am Hyun-Seok Kim, a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering and a leader pioneering AI innovation in healthcare. My passion lies in translating profound research in biosignal analysis – encompassing EEG (Electroencephalogram), PCG (Phonocardiogram), ABP (Arterial Blood Pressure), and ECG (Electrocardiogram) – into impactful AI solutions that tackle real-world clinical challenges and deliver tangible patient-centric value. As CTO of SignalHouse Co., Ltd., an AI healthcare startup, I have successfully led the entire product lifecycle – from initial conceptualization, AI model development, and defining reimbursement strategies, all the way through to regulatory approvals (KFDA), funding, and commercialization of AI-powered systems for intraoperative patient monitoring and prediction of postoperative complications. My academic and innovative contributions are recognized through numerous SCI-indexed publications, key patents successfully licensed for commercialization, and significant research grants. My ultimate goal is to accelerate the era of precision medicine through data and AI technology, ultimately enhancing human health. I invite you to explore my research, technology, and vision for a healthier future on this website
